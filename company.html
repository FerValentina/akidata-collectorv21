Capture and structuring

Focused on capturing and organizing information at its source, adding context, classification, and meaning to user-provided records.

AKI Integrator

Conectividad e interoperabilidad

Connectivity and interoperability

Orientado a conectar AKI con otros sistemas en ambas direcciones: incorporar información externa y compartir información con otras aplicaciones.

Focused on connecting AKI with other systems in both directions: bringing external information in and sharing information with other applications.
